About Kamea Tara

Your trusted partner for SME business registration and project proposals across Papua New Guinea.

Who We Are

Kamea Tara Business Consultant is a Papua New Guinea-based service dedicated to helping individuals, groups and small enterprises formalise their operations and access opportunities. We specialise in IPA business name and company registration, association registration, constitution writing, professional branding packages, and project proposal development.

Our work is built around a simple idea: formal registration and clear documentation should not be a barrier for ordinary Papua New Guineans who want to start a business, organise a community group, or seek funding for a development project. We exist to make those processes clearer, faster and more professional.

What We Stand For

We believe that many good ideas and genuine community efforts in PNG are held back by paperwork, distance from government offices, and a lack of practical guidance. Our role is to bridge that gap. We prepare documents that meet official requirements, present clients professionally, and give them a solid foundation for banking, contracting and funding applications.

We serve clients in Lae and, through our agent network and full online service, across the country — including a dedicated presence in Wewak Town for clients in East Sepik and surrounding areas.

Our Service Philosophy

How we approach every client engagement.

Clear Communication

We explain the process in plain language. You will know what documents are needed, what the next steps are, and what to expect. We prefer WhatsApp and phone for quick updates because that is how most clients prefer to communicate.

Complete Packages

Registration alone is rarely enough. A certificate without a logo, letterhead or clear profile still leaves a business looking incomplete. That is why our packages typically include branding and supporting materials — so you can start presenting yourself properly immediately.

Honest Advice

We help clients choose the structure that fits their real situation. Not every business needs a company. Not every group needs the most complex constitution. We advise based on goals, capacity and cost, not on selling the most expensive option.

Respect for Local Context

We understand the realities of operating in Papua New Guinea — distance, connectivity, community decision-making, and the importance of relationships. Our processes and documents are designed to work in this environment.
