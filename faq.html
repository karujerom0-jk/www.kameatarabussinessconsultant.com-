Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about business registration, association registration, TIN, proposals and how we work across Papua New Guinea.

What is included in the SME Business Name Registration package?
The package covers IPA Business Name Registration, IRC TIN Registration, and support for bank account opening profile documents. We prepare the required forms, guide you on documents needed, and lodge the applications on your behalf.
What is the difference between Business Name and Company Registration?
A Business Name (sole trader or partnership style) is simpler and faster. A Company (Pty Ltd style) creates a separate legal entity with limited liability and requires IPA company registration plus related IRC steps. We handle both and advise which suits your situation.
How long does Association (INC) registration take?
Association registration typically takes around 4 weeks. It includes IPA registration, IRC registration, required newspaper advertisements (Post Courier / National), and profile support for bank account opening.
Do you help with logo, letterhead and company profile?
Yes. Most of our registration packages include professional logo design and letterhead so you can present your business properly from day one. We also develop a basic business or association profile when required for bank or funding purposes.
Can you help with project proposals and funding applications?
Yes. We prepare Business Plans, Company Profiles, Project Proposals, SME Loan submissions, and Government & Donor funding applications. Common sectors include agriculture & livestock, fish farming, coffee, cocoa, oil palm, vanilla, tourism, solar, infrastructure, schools, churches, aid posts and community markets.
Do you serve clients outside Lae and Wewak?
Yes. Our service is nationwide. We assist many clients and SMEs across Papua New Guinea through online communication, document exchange and remote lodgement support. We also have a Wewak Town agent available.
What documents do I need to start?
Typical requirements include a valid ID (NID or passport), passport-size photos, residential address details, and for companies or associations the relevant constitutional or shareholder information. After you contact us we send a clear checklist for your specific package.
