Privacy Policy

How Kamea Tara Business Consultant collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: November 2026

Kamea Tara Business Consultant (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our services or website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Contact details you provide (name, phone number, email, WhatsApp number, address)
  • Identity and supporting documents needed for IPA, IRC, bank or funding applications
  • Business and project information you share for registration or proposal work
  • Messages and correspondence you send us

2. How we use your information

We use the information to:

  • Provide business name, company and association registration services
  • Prepare TIN applications, profiles, business plans and project proposals
  • Communicate with you about your matter
  • Lodge documents with relevant authorities (IPA, IRC, newspapers, banks) as instructed
  • Improve our services and respond to enquiries

3. Sharing of information

We only share information where necessary to deliver the services you requested — for example with the Investment Promotion Authority (IPA), Internal Revenue Commission (IRC), newspapers for required advertisements, or banks when supporting account opening. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide. Documents and contact details are handled confidentially and used only for the purpose of your engagement with us.

5. Retention

We keep records for as long as needed to complete your matter and for reasonable business and legal record-keeping purposes.

6. Your choices

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. You may also ask us to stop using your details for marketing communications (we currently focus on service-related contact).
