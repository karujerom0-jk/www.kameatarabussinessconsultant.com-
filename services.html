1. Business Name Registration

Ideal for Sole Traders & Partnerships

Business Name Registration is the most common and accessible way for individuals and small partnerships to formalise their trading name in Papua New Guinea. Once registered with the Investment Promotion Authority (IPA), your business name is protected and you can open a bank account, issue invoices, and apply for many forms of support under that name.

At Kamea Tara we manage the entire process so you do not have to navigate government forms alone. We prepare the application, advise on name availability, and coordinate the related documents that make your business look professional from the start.

What is Included

✓ IPA Business Name Registration
✓ IRC TIN Registration
✓ Professional Logo Design
✓ Letterhead Design
✓ Business Profile Development
✓ Account Opening Assistance

Typical Steps We Follow

  1. Discuss your business activity and preferred trading name
  2. Check name availability and advise on alternatives if needed
  3. Prepare and lodge the IPA Business Name application
  4. Assist with IRC TIN application
  5. Design logo and letterhead to match your brand
  6. Develop a concise business profile
  7. Guide you on bank account requirements once certificates are ready
Who should choose Business Name Registration? Sole traders, family businesses, small retail and service operators, market vendors expanding, and anyone who wants a simple, lower-cost formal structure without the complexity of a limited company.

2. Company Registration

Limited Liability & Stronger Credibility

Registering a company creates a separate legal entity. This structure is preferred by many entrepreneurs who plan to grow, seek larger contracts, work with government or corporate clients, or want the protection of limited liability. A company also presents a more formal image to banks, suppliers and partners.

Kamea Tara handles the full company incorporation process with IPA and the related IRC requirements, while also delivering the branding and profile materials that help your new company look established from day one.

What is Included

✓ IPA Company Registration
✓ IRC TIN Certificate
✓ Logo Design
✓ Letterhead Design
✓ Company Profile Development

Key Benefits of a Company Structure

  • Limited liability protection for shareholders in most situations
  • Stronger credibility when bidding for contracts or partnerships
  • Easier to bring in additional shareholders or investors later
  • Clear separation between personal and business assets
  • Preferred structure for many formal funding and procurement processes

What We Need From You

  1. Proposed company name (and alternatives)
  2. Details of directors and shareholders
  3. Registered office address
  4. Nature of business activities
  5. Share capital structure (we can advise on simple structures)

4. Constitution Write-Up

Business Groups • Societies • Associations

A clear constitution is essential for any group that wants to operate formally, open a bank account in the group’s name, receive grants, or register with IPA. A good constitution sets out the purpose of the group, membership rules, leadership structure, decision-making processes, and how funds are managed.

We prepare practical constitutions that meet IPA expectations while remaining understandable to members. We avoid overly complex legal language and focus on rules that actually help the group function smoothly and reduce the risk of internal disputes.

Types of Constitutions We Prepare

✓ Business Group Constitutions
✓ Corporate Society Rules
✓ Association Constitutions

Typical Contents of a Constitution We Draft

  • Name and objectives of the group or association
  • Membership eligibility, admission and cessation
  • Office bearers, their roles and election processes
  • Meetings (annual general meeting, executive meetings, special meetings)
  • Quorum and voting procedures
  • Financial management and banking arrangements
  • Amendment procedures
  • Dissolution and distribution of assets
Important: A constitution is a living document. We encourage groups to discuss the draft with members before finalising so that everyone understands and accepts the rules. This reduces future conflict.

5. Association Registration

Full Package for Formal Associations

Association registration is ideal for community groups, women’s associations, youth groups, church-based organisations, cooperatives and other collective bodies that want to operate with official recognition. Registration allows the association to hold assets, open bank accounts, enter into agreements and apply for funding in its own name.

The process involves more steps than a simple business name, including public advertisement for objections. We manage the full sequence so your group can focus on its mission rather than paperwork.

Complete Association Package Includes

✓ Lodgement and IPA Certificates
✓ Advertisement for Objection or Rejection
✓ IRC TIN
✓ Logo and Letterhead Design
✓ Profile Development
✓ Constitution Write-Up
✓ Account Opening Support

Overview of the Association Registration Journey

  1. Initial discussion about the purpose and membership of the association
  2. Drafting of the constitution and collection of member details
  3. Preparation of all IPA application documents
  4. Lodgement and coordination of the required public advertisement
  5. Follow-up until certificates are issued
  6. TIN registration and branding package delivery
  7. Guidance on opening the association bank account

Whether your group is newly formed or has been operating informally for years, we can help bring it under a proper legal structure that protects members and opens doors to formal opportunities.
